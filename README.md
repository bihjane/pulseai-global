# PulseAI Global

A digital clinic concept for the United States first: understand your health data in plain words, follow your care on a journey map, and meet verified clinicians online.

Product owner: Charles Sonnet. Design, prototype and documents prepared by Dr. Janet Shufor Bih with Claude. Status: prototype, 22 November 2026. Nothing here is live, and no real patient data should be entered.

## What is in this repository

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `site/index.html` | Marketing website with Start flow, 37 language picker, wellness section and investor page. One self-contained file. |
| `app/index.html` | Working app prototype for patients and clinicians. One self-contained file. Data stays in the browser. |
| `docs/BUILD_SPEC.md` | Build specification for developers: roles, screens, data fields, rules, services. |
| `docs/US_LAUNCH_RULES.md` | United States telemedicine, privacy and prescribing rules, with sources, for a lawyer to confirm. |
| `docs/ONE_PAGER.md` | One-page summary for partners and funders. |
| `docs/PITCH_DECK.md` | Slide-by-slide pitch outline. |
| `docs/PLACEHOLDERS.md` | Every open question and placeholder that Charles needs to answer. |

## Placeholders

Anything in square brackets, such as `[FEE]`, is a placeholder. The full list is in `docs/PLACEHOLDERS.md` and inside the app under "Open questions for the product owner".

## Important limits

- The symptom guidance and the reading explanations are simple demonstration rules. A licensed clinical lead must review them before any real use.
- The practitioners are invented samples. Visits, payments and prescriptions are simulated.
- The investor page is a design preview and not an offer of securities.
- The legal research is a starting point for a lawyer and not legal advice.
