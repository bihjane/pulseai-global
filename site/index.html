Wellness and prevention

Feeling fine? Let us keep it that way

You do not need to be sick to use PulseAI. Build a picture of your health while you are well, spot changes early, and get friendly guidance from nutritionists, nurses and doctors.

Know your baseline

Record your usual blood pressure, weight, sleep and lab values so a change stands out early.

A prevention journey map

Check ups, screenings and vaccines your clinician recommends, laid out as a simple timeline with reminders.

Habits that hold

Small weekly goals for food, movement, sleep and stress, with a nutritionist or coach when you want one.

Family and future

Note your family history and share it before a visit, so advice fits you and the people you care for.

Why PulseAI

The problem

Health records are scattered, results are written in jargon, and a trusted clinician is often far away or hard to afford.

The product

Plain language analytics, a journey map of each person's care, and an open marketplace of verified clinicians, in one app.

The model

Proposed: a platform fee paid by independent practitioners, optional memberships for patients, and plans for clinics, employers and sponsors.

The edge

Built for many languages, weak connections and local payments from day one, with a verified practitioner database at the core.
