# PulseAI: understand your health, your way

**A digital clinic that explains your health in plain words, maps your care journey, and connects you with verified clinicians.**

## The problem
People receive lab results and readings they cannot interpret. Their records sit in different places. Reaching a trusted clinician takes time and money, and each visit starts from zero.

## The product
- **Understand:** every number explained in three parts: what it is, what yours means, what to do next.
- **Journey map:** symptom checks, readings, visits and prescriptions on one timeline, with the next step highlighted.
- **Verified clinicians, on their own hours:** doctors, nurses, nutritionists and therapists apply, are verified, and switch themselves to "free now". Patients see only people licensed in their state.
- **Wellness and prevention:** baselines, reminders and habits for people who are well.

## What exists today

## Why now
Consumer health apps explain data, and telehealth apps deliver visits. PulseAI joins the two with an open, verified clinician marketplace.
